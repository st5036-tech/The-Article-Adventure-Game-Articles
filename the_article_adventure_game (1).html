Museum Grammar Archives

1. Article 'A'

Used before singular countable nouns that start with a consonant sound.

  • I have a dog.
  • She is a teacher.
  • Phonetic Rule: She studies at a university. (Starts with consonant sound /juː/).

2. Article 'AN'

Used before singular countable nouns that start with a vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u).

  • She ate an apple.
  • He is an engineer.
  • Phonetic Rule: Waited for an hour / an honest person. (The 'H' is silent!).

3. Article 'THE' (Definite)

Used for specific, unique, or previously mentioned nouns.

  • Please close the door of the classroom.
  • The sun is shining today. (Unique object).
  • Where is the book I left on your desk? (Specific item).
